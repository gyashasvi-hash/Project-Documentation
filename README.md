# **GUEST FEEDBACK & REVIEW PORTAL**

### *A Web-Based Hotel Guest Satisfaction and Review Management System*

---

## **01. ABOUT THE PROJECT**

The Guest Feedback & Review Portal is a web-based application designed to make it easy for hotel guests to share their experiences and help hotel management improve service quality.

Guests can submit ratings and written comments about their stay through a simple online interface. Visitors can browse published reviews to learn about other guests' experiences before choosing a hotel.

Hotel staff can access a management dashboard to view submitted feedback, review rating summaries, and identify frequently mentioned concerns.

## **02. PROJECT OBJECTIVES**

- To collect guest feedback and ratings.
- To improve hotel service quality.
- To help visitors explore guest reviews.
- To provide hotel staff with useful feedback insights.

## **03. KEY FEATURES**

- Guest feedback submission
- Hotel ratings and written reviews
- Public review browsing
- Staff management dashboard
- Rating summaries
- Identification of common guest concerns

## **04. PROJECT OVERVIEW**

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Users | Hotel guests, public visitors, authorised hotel staff |
| Main Features | Ratings, reviews, dashboard, rating summaries |
| Expected Outcome | A centralised platform for collecting and analysing guest feedback |
